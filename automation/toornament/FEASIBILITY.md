# Toornament browser automation feasibility

Research date: 2026-10-31. The owner approved the Kochi tournament `2596029129977786367` as a testing tournament and explicitly approved using its signed-in Alive De organizer account. One match result was changed there. No paid API, private endpoint replay or production deployment was used.

**Decision: supervised operations feasible; no production go.** On the approved Kochi tournament, result entry, participant creation, forfeit, an unlocked reset and the downstream lock/unlock sequence succeeded through the organizer UI. Public readback of the first 2–1 result and advancement succeeded. A saved Playwright session reopened the approved tournament in a fresh browser process. Recorder behavior, session lifetime, safe pacing and website automation permission remain unverified. This is not a production or legal approval.

## Proposed operating envelope, not a certified limit

Use one browser, one serialized job and one pending write at a time. As an initial conservative **local testing choice**, leave at least 5 seconds between completed mutations and require a successful UI readback before the next. This is not an official rate limit, a measured safe rate or evidence of permission. Do not load-test the service. Stop on throttling, challenges, denied access, ambiguous identity or unexpected state; do not rotate accounts or evade controls.

Use labels/roles and freshly observed DOM evidence before choosing locators. Save actual identity and before/after assertions, not guessed selectors. Keep all long numeric Toornament IDs as strings. A browser timeout after Save means an unknown outcome until readback, not permission to click Save again.

## Go/no-go gate

Proceed to a supervised disposable-fixture pilot only after current website terms and account scope are resolved. A technical go additionally requires the matrix above to pass with captured evidence on both 8- and 16-participant fixtures, unambiguous match identity, duplicate-safe behavior, successful readback, and fail-closed lock/session handling. Any prohibited automation, unresolved identity, unhandled uncertain write or required control bypass is a no-go. No production recommendation, selector stability claim, session-duration promise or rate certification is made here.
